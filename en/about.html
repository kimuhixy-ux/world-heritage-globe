Purpose of this site

World Heritage Globe is an independently operated PWA for learning and browsing that plots all 1,258 UNESCO World Heritage sites (as of 2026) on a rotating 3D globe. It supports filtering by category (Cultural / Natural / Mixed), inscription year, and World Heritage in Danger status, plus a visited-sites tracker. Site data is built from information published by the UNESCO World Heritage Centre (whc.unesco.org), and the globe texture uses satellite imagery released by NASA (Blue Marble / Black Marble).
